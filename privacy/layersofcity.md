# LAYERSofCITY – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026 · App: LAYERSofCITY (layersofcity.com) · Developer: MrTCgL · Contact: nabkap.app@gmail.com

LAYERSofCITY is a map that helps newcomers get oriented in a city. It has no accounts, no ads, no cookies and no tracking SDKs.

**Data we collect:** We do not collect personal data and we do not sell or share it.

**Stored on your device only:** Theme, language, map style, layer settings and the places/notes you save are kept in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device. Clear the site data (or uninstall) to delete them.

**Location:** Only when you tap "My location", your device's location is used to show a dot on the map. It is processed on your device and is not sent or stored by us. You can deny or revoke the permission at any time.

**Anonymous usage statistics:** We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookieless and does not identify you. It records aggregate page views (page, referrer, browser/device type, country) to see how the site is used.

**Third-party services:** To show the map and live info, your device downloads public data directly from these services, which, like any website, receive your IP address and the requested map area or city: OpenFreeMap and OpenStreetMap (map tiles, map notes), Esri/ArcGIS (satellite imagery), Open-Meteo (weather for the city), Frankfurter (exchange rates), GitHub Pages (hosting) and Cloudflare (analytics). We do not send them any personal information. The "Directions" button opens Google Maps, where Google's privacy policy applies.

**Retention and deletion:** We hold no personal data, so there is nothing to delete on our side. On-device data stays until you delete it.

**Children:** The app is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.

**Changes:** Updates are published at this address with a new effective date.

**Contact:** nabkap.app@gmail.com
