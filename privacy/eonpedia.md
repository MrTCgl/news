# EONpedia – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026 · App: EONpedia (Android app and the website eonpedia.com) · Developer: MrTCgL · Contact: nabkap.app@gmail.com

EONpedia is a bilingual (English/Turkish) visual timeline of history, art, philosophy and architecture, from the Big Bang to the present. The Android app opens the same content as the website eonpedia.com.

## Summary

There are **no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no cookies and no tracking across apps or websites**. We do not collect personal data such as your name, e-mail, location, contacts or photos, and we do not sell or share data. The app contains no advertising SDKs and no Firebase or Google Analytics.

## Data the app uses

| Data | Why | Where it stays |
|---|---|---|
| Preferences: light/dark theme, your bookmarks and your star ratings of events | Remember your choices | Only on your device (browser storage). Never sent to our servers. |
| Scroll position (temporary) | Return you to the same place after switching language or section | Only on your device, cleared when the session ends |
| Anonymous usage statistics: pages viewed, and actions such as opening an event, switching language, using filters (only counts) or sharing (only the method and the page link) | See which content is read and improve the app | Aggregated by our analytics providers (see below). No cookies, no advertising ID, no account. |

**Permissions:** Internet only. The app does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage or notification access.

## Third-party service providers

- **Vercel** – hosts the app and website; **Vercel Web Analytics** counts page views and the anonymous actions above without cookies. Visitors are not identified; the provider uses a short-lived hash that is discarded within 24 hours.
- **Cloudflare** – serves images and DNS; **Cloudflare Web Analytics** counts page views without cookies or fingerprinting.
- **OpenStreetMap** – when you open an event that has a location, a small map is loaded from openstreetmap.org. Only the map area of that event is requested.

Like any internet connection, these providers see your IP address and device/browser type to deliver the content and process them briefly under their own privacy policies. We do not receive or keep these logs.

When you tap a link to another site (e.g. Google Maps, an image source such as Wikimedia Commons, Layers of City) or share an event to X or WhatsApp, that service's privacy policy applies.

## Retention and deletion

We store no personal data on our servers, so there is nothing to delete there. Analytics data is kept only in aggregated, anonymous form. Data on your device is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Questions or requests: nabkap.app@gmail.com (we reply within 30 days).

## Children

EONpedia is educational content suitable for a general audience, but it is not directed at children under 13. It does not ask anyone for personal information.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at this address and the effective date above is updated.
