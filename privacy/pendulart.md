# PendulArt – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026 · App: PendulArt (Android, package `com.mrtcgl.pendulart`) · Developer: MrTCgL · Contact: nabkap.app@gmail.com

PendulArt lets you create abstract paintings with a simulated paint pendulum.

## Data we collect

**None.** PendulArt does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking or third-party SDKs. The app does not connect to the internet and does not request the Internet permission.

## Data stored on your device

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Your saved works (image, small preview and the settings used to make them) | Show them under "My works" |
| Preferences (language, light/dark theme, last studio settings) | Remember your choices |

This data stays only on your device. Deleting a work in the app removes it; uninstalling the app removes all of it. If you have Android's own device backup turned on, Android may include app data in your personal Google backup; this is controlled by you and Google, and we have no access to it.

## Photos and sharing

- **Save to gallery:** when you tap Save, the picture is written to your device's photo gallery. The app only adds images; it does not read your photos. (On Android 9 and older the storage permission is used for this.)
- **Share:** when you tap Share, the picture is passed to the Android share sheet and goes only to the app you choose.

## Third parties

No personal data is sent to us or to any third party. The app uses only on-device open-source libraries (Flutter, shared_preferences, gal, share_plus, image), none of which collect data.

## Children

PendulArt is a general-audience creative app and is not directed at children under 13. Since no data is collected, no data from children is collected either.

## Changes

If a future version adds online features (for example optional cloud backup or in-app purchases through Google Play), this policy will be updated at this address before that version is released.

nabkap.app@gmail.com
