# TRaLIST – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026 · App: TRaLIST (Android, package `com.tralist.mobile`) · Developer: MrTCgL · Contact: nabkap.app@gmail.com

TRaLIST is a Borsa Istanbul stock screening and analysis app. It uses the same account and server as the website temelrasyoanalist.com.

## Data we collect

| Data | Why | Where |
|---|---|---|
| E-mail address and password | Create your account, sign in, verify e-mail, reset password. The password is stored only as a one-way hash (bcrypt). | Our server |
| Your app content: filters, watchlist, notes, portfolio entries (stock, quantity, cost, transactions, capital), score formula, alert rules | Provide the service and keep the app and website in sync. You enter this yourself; it is not linked to any brokerage account. | Our server |
| Security and session info: IP address, device/browser type (user agent), the last 10 sign-ins, sign-in count, session duration | Protect your account, prevent unauthorized use and account sharing. | Our server |
| Subscription status (trial / paid / expiry date) | Manage access to paid features. No payments are processed in the app. | Our server |
| Push notification token and device build ID (only if you turn notifications on) | Send the watchlist and portfolio alerts you asked for. Deleted when you turn notifications off or sign out. | Our server |
| Sign-in token, last e-mail, theme and list view settings | Keep you signed in and remember your preferences. | Only on your device (encrypted storage) |

We do **not** access location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or files. The app contains **no ads, no analytics and no tracking SDKs**. We do not sell your data or share it for advertising or marketing.

**Permissions:** Internet (to load market data and your account) and Notifications (optional, for alerts).

## Third-party service providers

Data is sent only over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and only to run the service:

- **MongoDB Atlas** – database (account and app content)
- **Render** – server hosting
- **Resend** – sending verification e-mails
- **Expo Push Service** and **Google Firebase Cloud Messaging** – delivering push notifications (push token and notification text only)

Their servers may be located outside your country. Market data is fetched by our server from public sources; your personal data is not sent to them.

## Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time:

- In the app: Settings → Delete my account
- Without the app: https://temelrasyoanalist.com/hesap-sil
- Or by e-mail to nabkap.app@gmail.com

Deletion removes your account and all data listed above from our database immediately. Daily backups kept for error recovery are erased automatically within 30 days. Data stored on your device is removed when you sign out or uninstall the app.

## Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its processing (including your rights under the Turkish KVKK and the GDPR where applicable) by writing to nabkap.app@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

## Children

TRaLIST is intended for adults (18+). It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at this address and the effective date above is updated.

## Üçüncü taraf hizmet sağlayıcılar

Veriler yalnızca hizmetin çalışması için ve şifreli bağlantı (HTTPS) ile iletilir:

- **MongoDB Atlas** – veritabanı (hesap ve içerik)
- **Render** – sunucu barındırma
- **Resend** – doğrulama e-postalarının gönderimi
- **Expo Push Service** ve **Google Firebase Cloud Messaging** – bildirimlerin iletilmesi (yalnızca bildirim anahtarı ve bildirim metni)

Bu sağlayıcıların sunucuları yurt dışında olabilir. Piyasa verileri sunucumuz tarafından herkese açık kaynaklardan alınır; kişisel verileriniz bu kaynaklara gönderilmez.

## Saklama ve silme

Verileriniz hesabınız açık olduğu sürece saklanır. Hesabınızı istediğiniz an silebilirsiniz:

- Uygulamada: Ayarlar → Hesabımı sil
- Uygulama olmadan: https://temelrasyoanalist.com/hesap-sil
- Ya da nabkap.app@gmail.com adresine yazarak

Silme ile hesabınız ve yukarıdaki tüm veriler veritabanından hemen silinir. Hatalara karşı tutulan günlük yedekler en geç 30 gün içinde kendiliğinden silinir. Cihazdaki veriler çıkış yapınca ya da uygulama kaldırılınca silinir.

## Haklarınız

KVKK m.11 (ve uygulanabildiği ölçüde GDPR) kapsamında verilerinize erişme, düzeltme, silme ve işlemeye itiraz haklarınızı nabkap.app@gmail.com adresine yazarak kullanabilirsiniz. En geç 30 gün içinde yanıtlanır.
