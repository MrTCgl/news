# LingoBall: roll into words – Privacy Policy
Last updated: 24 November 2026 · Package: com.nabkap.lingoball · Contact: nabkap.app@gmail.com

**Data we collect:** None. LingoBall does not collect, store on servers, sell or share personal data. No accounts, ads, analytics or third-party tracking SDKs.

**On-device data:** Settings, word notebook, daily counters and statistics are stored only on your device and are deleted when the app is uninstalled.

**Network:** The app may download a small public announcement file from this repository. No personal information or identifiers are sent.

**Sensors and notifications:** The motion sensor is used only to steer the ball; its data never leaves the device. The optional daily reminder is a local notification.

**Children:** The app is not directed at children under 13.

**Changes:** Updates are published at this address.
