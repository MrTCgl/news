# LingoBall – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 6 November 2026_

LingoBall ("the app") is a calm language-learning game for Android, made by MrTCgL (NabKap). This page explains what happens to your data when you use it.

## Short version

LingoBall does **not** collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking.

## What stays on your phone

The app saves these only on your device, in its own private storage:

- your language choices, sound and vibration settings
- the words you caught and the words you marked as known
- your daily progress (words caught and seen per level) and your daily goal

This information never leaves your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

## Internet

When it starts, the app downloads a small public file with an optional announcement from `raw.githubusercontent.com` (GitHub). No information about you is sent with this request. Like any website, GitHub may see your IP address as part of the connection; see [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app may show at most one reminder a day about your daily goal. The reminder is scheduled on your phone; no server is involved. You can turn it off with the bell in the word book or in your phone's settings.

## Text-to-speech

Words are read aloud by your phone's built-in text-to-speech engine, on your device.

## Children

The app is suitable for everyone and does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

# LingoBall – Gizlilik Politikası

_Son güncelleme: 6 Kasım 2026_

LingoBall, MrTCgL (NabKap) tarafından geliştirilen sakin bir dil öğrenme oyunudur.

- **Kişisel veri toplamaz.** Hesap, reklam, analiz veya takip yoktur. Hiçbir veri satılmaz ya da paylaşılmaz.
- **Telefonda kalanlar:** dil seçimleri, ses ve titreşim ayarları, yakalanan ve "biliyorum" işaretlenen kelimeler, günlük ilerleme ve hedef. Bunlar yalnızca cihazınızda tutulur. Uygulama silinince bunlar da silinir.

- **Bildirim:** izin verirseniz günlük hedef için günde en fazla bir hatırlatma gösterilir. Hatırlatma telefonunuzda planlanır. Defterdeki zil simgesinden kapatılabilir.
- **Sesli okuma:** kelimeleri telefonunuzun kendi metin okuma motoru okur.
